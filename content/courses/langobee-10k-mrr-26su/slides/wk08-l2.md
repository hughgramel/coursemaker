# The three funnel rates

---

## Rate 1: visitor-to-signup

Where does your visitor-to-signup benchmark come from?

Carter (2024) and RevenueCat (2025) benchmarks for consumer subscription apps:
- Organic/community cold traffic: 2-5% visitor-to-signup typical
- Pre-warmed audience (creator referral, prior list): 5-10%
- CC-required trial: lower end of the cold range, typically 2-3%

LangoBee's CC-required 14-day trial sits at the lower end. A realistic planning assumption: **2-4%** visitor-to-signup for cold traffic.

Direct/Discord traffic (LangoBee's only proven channel) likely converts higher, around 5-10%, because those visitors are pre-warmed.

(Carter, 2024; RevenueCat Research Team, 2025)

---

## Rate 2: signup-to-trial

The gap between signup and trial start is the paywall moment.

RevenueCat (2025): overall median download-to-paid rate is 1.7% at 30 days, with top apps reaching 4.2%. The implied signup-to-trial rate for CC-required trials lands in the 15-40% range when trials convert at the reported 35-40% median.

For a CC-required trial with an early paywall placement:
- Ambitious estimate: 40% of signups start a trial
- Conservative estimate: 15-25%

LangoBee benchmark: zero trial starts from all signups to date (June 2026 snapshot). This rate is the single least trustworthy input in the whole model.

(RevenueCat Research Team, 2025; LangoBee June 2026 snapshot)

---

## Rate 3: trial-to-paid

RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps 2025:
- CC-required trials: median 35-40% conversion to paid
- Top performers (trials longer than 4 days): above 60%
- Overall download-to-paid in 30 days: median 1.7%, top apps 4.2%

The 35-40% figure applies after a user has started a trial. It does not include the funnel loss from signup to trial start.

Education apps specifically: refund rate 4.86%, which slightly reduces net trial-to-paid.

Planning assumption for LangoBee: **35%** base case, **25%** pessimistic.

(RevenueCat Research Team, 2025)

# Three scenarios

---

<!-- _class: two-col -->

## Scenario A: community-led

Reddit (r/Spanish, r/languagelearning) and Discord.

**Assumed rates:**
- Visitor-to-signup: 3-5%
- Signup-to-trial: 20-30%
- Trial-to-paid: 35%

**Required monthly visitors:**
To generate 63 new paying subs/mo:
- Pessimistic: 63 / (0.03 x 0.20 x 0.35) = ~30,000
- Optimistic: 63 / (0.05 x 0.30 x 0.35) = ~12,000

**Today's baseline:** 174 direct visitors/month.

**Gap:** 70-170x current volume.

## Scenario B: creator-led

One comprehensible-input YouTuber (50K-200K subs) per month.

**Assumed rates:**
- Visitor-to-signup: 5-8%
- Signup-to-trial: 35%
- Trial-to-paid: 40%

**Required monthly visitors:**
- Pessimistic: 63 / (0.05 x 0.35 x 0.40) = ~9,000
- Optimistic: 63 / (0.08 x 0.35 x 0.40) = ~5,600

**Channel math:** a 100K-sub creator video at 1% click-through = 1,000 visitors. Requires 5-9 creator videos per month, or a large creator.

---

## Scenario C: loop-led

The shorts share loop: a learner shares a clip, the share drives new visitors.

**Assumed rates:**
- Visitor-to-signup: 2-4% (cold share context)
- Signup-to-trial: 20%
- Trial-to-paid: 30%

**Required monthly visitors:**
- Pessimistic: 63 / (0.02 x 0.20 x 0.30) = ~52,500
- Optimistic: 63 / (0.04 x 0.20 x 0.30) = ~26,250

**Today's baseline:** 408 shorts impressions, 1 share in 30 days (June 2026 snapshot).

The loop is unvalidated. At current share rates, this scenario requires ~130x more impressions than LangoBee currently generates.

(LangoBee June 2026 snapshot)

---

## Scenario comparison

```
Scenario        Visitors/mo (pessim.) Visitors/mo (optim.)
--------------  ---------------------  --------------------
Community-led   ~30,000                ~12,000
Creator-led     ~9,000                 ~5,600
Loop-led        ~52,500                ~26,250
```

Creator-led has the lowest visitor requirement because the channel pre-filters for intent. Community-led and loop-led have lower conversion rates because they reach colder audiences.

None of these scenarios is achievable with LangoBee's current acquisition (174 visitors/month). The model quantifies the gap; the next three weeks identify which scenario closes it fastest.

---

## Why 5% churn caps all three scenarios

Generating more traffic does not change the churn ceiling. At 5% monthly churn:

```
Ceiling = New subs/month / 0.05
```

In scenario B (optimistic, 63 new subs/month from 5,600 visitors):
```
Ceiling = 63 / 0.05 = 1,260 subscribers = ~$10,067 MRR
```

If churn rises to 8% (early cohort typical):
```
Ceiling = 63 / 0.08 = 788 subscribers = ~$6,295 MRR
```

$10K MRR becomes unreachable regardless of traffic until churn falls below 5%.

Carter (2024): top-quartile consumer subscription apps retain about 50% of subscribers after year 1 (implied annual churn ~50%, or roughly 6% monthly). Reaching the sub-5% range requires deliberate retention work from day 1.

(Carter, 2024; Rachitsky, 2022)

## Maximum defensible CAC per scenario

Using RevenueCat median LTV of $10.97 and 3:1 rule: max CAC = **$3.66**.
Using P90 LTV of $52.41: max CAC = **$17.47**.

**Community-led CAC estimate:**
5 hrs/month founder time at $50/hr = $250 total.
At 63 new subs: $250 / 63 = **$3.97/sub** (slightly above the $3.66 floor; within the base case $17.47).

**Creator-led CAC estimate:**
$200 micro-sponsorship per month.
At 63 new subs: $200 / 63 = **$3.17/sub** (under even the conservative floor).

**Loop-led CAC estimate:**
2 hrs/month at $50/hr = $100 total.
At 63 new subs: $100 / 63 = **$1.59/sub** (lowest CAC, but requires the highest traffic).

## Take-aways

1. Visitor-to-signup rate is 2-8% for LangoBee depending on channel warmth; signup-to-trial is the least-known and highest-variance rate in the model.
2. Creator-led acquisition requires the fewest monthly visitors (5,600-9,000) to generate 63 new paying subscribers per month.
3. Community-led and loop-led scenarios need 12,000-52,500 monthly visitors, compared to 174 today.
4. 5% monthly churn caps steady-state MRR at 1,260 x ARPA regardless of how many visitors you generate. Retention work is not a nice-to-have; it is the binding constraint.
5. At the RevenueCat median LTV, the maximum defensible CAC is $3.66-$17.47. Creator-led and loop-led scenarios stay within this range; community-led is marginal.
